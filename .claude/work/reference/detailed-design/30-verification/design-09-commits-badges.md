# Verification report: design unit 09-commits-badges

Verifier: independent (not the author). Date: 2026-10-14. Document: `20-design/09-commits-badges.md` and its companion `20-design/09-commits-badges.assets/` (the reference prototype). Fixes were made directly in the document and in the assets; the document ends with a "Verification log" section, and fixes carry V-nn markers in the text.

Working copy: the canonical prototype `20-design/09-commits-badges.assets/` (module `github.com/cantara/source-code-portal`, Go 1.27.1, darwin/arm64), rebuilt against the **current** sibling assets of units 01, 03, 06 and 07 (the author's first copies had drifted, V-04). Scratch material (original document, mutation scripts, the font download) is under the session scratchpad `verify-09-commits-badges/`.

## Result

23 findings (V-01 to V-25; V-11 and V-21 withdrawn): 20 fixed (V-24 by a workaround inside this unit), 1 accepted (V-22), 2 open (V-23, V-25; both cross-unit). Severity: 2 high, 15 medium, 6 low. Q-3 (Camo) and the planned integration tests remain open as well. Verdict: **ready with caveats**. The design is complete (all 15 sections present and concrete). After the fixes the prototype builds, vets, passes `go test -race` (52 top-level tests, 113 with subtests; coverage 85.7 % `commits`, 81.8 % `badges`), both fuzz targets run clean, and the width generator reproduces its output byte for byte. The two high findings were real defects in the job design (a case-unfolded job key; a head-sweep skip that swallowed webhook pushes) and are fixed with tests that were shown to fail when the fix is removed.

## Facts confirmed by the verifier

* DejaVu Sans 2.37 archive and font hashes; the generator output is byte-identical to `widths_gen.go`; the independent Python table reader agrees for every golden string.
* Fuzz: `FuzzMerge` 905,956 and `FuzzRenderWellFormed` 1,153,824 executions in 10 s each on the final code, no finding.
* The 19 badge goldens, the day-grouping vectors and the width vectors are reproducible.

## Deviations proposed (recorded in section 14 of the document)

D-4 (cursor and total order include the slug and SHA descending; V-08), D-9 and Q-2 closed, D-12 (`httpapi/openapi` import, with the alias alternative), D-8 reworded. No ADR is contradicted. No rate-limit deviation (V-25 withdrawn into a question).

## Unresolved

1. V-23 / Q-13: units 03 and 04 must agree on what the planner knows; this unit works either way.
2. V-25 / Q-11: unit 06 to confirm the `badges` burst for shared egress addresses.
3. V-24 / Q-12: catalogue (unit 08) to decide whether `catalog.repo` keys are folded (06 Q12).
4. Q-3: Camo behaviour (redirects, `max-age` floor, 404 caching) is unverified; direct serving is the design default and H4 resolves it with a real README.
5. The planned tests (`TestListCommitsConformance`, Playwright width check, the integration tests of 12.5 including the push-within-seconds and corrupt-ring restart tests) cannot run until the engine, the real adapter, the SPA and `cmd/fakegithub` exist.
