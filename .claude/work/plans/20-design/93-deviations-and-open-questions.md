## 1. How to read this document

- Status ACCEPT: unit-local deviation, no cross-unit conflict, becomes part of the design as written in the unit. DEFAULT: open question; the unit default is adopted. CLOSED: answered by this review. DEVIATION: this review accepts a change against a unit or the architecture (an amendment row exists). OWNER: needs the owner; the recommendation is the default that ships. REJECT: proposal not adopted for v1. OPEN: cannot be settled on paper.
- Section 2 holds the cross-unit decisions of this review. Section 3 lists owner-confirmation items. Section 4 is the full index of all 549 rows with a recommendation each.

Row counts by status: ACCEPT 289, CLOSED 2, DEFAULT 235, DEVIATION 1, OPEN 4, OWNER 15, REJECT 3.
